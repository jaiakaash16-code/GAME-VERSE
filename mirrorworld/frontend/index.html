HOW TO PLAY

◐ Move

A / D or ← / → — walk
Space or ↑ — jump
W — flip to Mirror world
S or ↓ — flip back to Normal
R — restart level

⬛ Rules

There are two worlds stacked on top of each other. Anything you press, open, or move affects both worlds simultaneously.

🚪 Doors

Each world has its own doors and buttons. A button pressed in World A still keeps the door open when you flip to World B.

☠ Hazards

Spikes only exist in one world. Step into the wrong one and you'll be killed — but the other world stays safe.

✦ Goal

Reach the glowing tile. Plan your flips. Every action echoes.

🏆 Score

Faster times, fewer deaths, fewer flips = higher score. Submit to the leaderboard from the SCORE tab.
